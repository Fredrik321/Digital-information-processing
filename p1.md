### Format, array shape, data type, units, range and byte size
All of the images are in a .jpg format
The images are classified into 6 different categories of bird species, the dataset containing 129 and 143 images in each class. With the dataset containg a total of 811 images.
All images have a shape of (224,244,3)
The datatype is uint8 
The units show pixel intensity, ranges between 0-255
Byte size can be calculated to be 811 * 224 * 224 * 3: equalling a byte size of 122,036,736
### Sampling rate/spacing
For the dataset exact details about the images are not provided, however some details can be ascertained from looking at the images. All of the images have a resolution 224 x 224 which are stored in 3 color channels. Exact distance and angle from the camera are unctrolled and are not noted down. However they distance seems varied and most of the pictures are taken from a side angle. While images seem to been genine pictures taken of the birds, some are also drawings. overall due to many details such camera distance, angle and lens are unrecorded makes it not possible to have an exact sampling rate.
